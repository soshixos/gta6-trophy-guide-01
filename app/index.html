Roadmap to the Platinum

One playthrough is enough. Play the story first and let the side content come to you; the checklist only opens after the credits, but everything you do earlier already counts toward it. Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap, OutputLag trophy list.

  1. Phase 1 — Story first Chapters 1–5 · 60–80 hours

    Finish the story. That earns the 12 hidden chapter and mission trophies, including the gold for the final mission. Do optional missions as they appear — 100 of them count toward 100% completion — and scan vehicles and study animals as you go. Before Chapter 1 ends, do Lucia’s balcony meditation (see #28). Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

    Earns: #2–#13

  2. Phase 2 — Side strands Strangers, Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret

    Finish the character finales — Andy (Lucia-only), Kenton (7 shipwrecks), Billy, Randy (16 sports cars), Tyrone (Jason-only), Bret (Jason-only hunting) — and 8 stranger strands. Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete are confirmed to count toward Strangers & Freaks; Andy, Billy and Tyrone have their own trophies and do not count. Yago’s Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 is time-sensitive, so save first and race when it appears.

    Earns: #22–#27, #29, #25

  3. Phase 3 — Activities, collectibles & robberies Unlocks in Chapters 2–4

    Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win a Top Fun drone race at all 14 locations, win 20 different races, finish an arcade game, beat Cal at fishing, meditate at all 18 spots. Collect: 3 card sets, WAiNK scans at 100 spots, 100 vehicles. Rob: a jewelry store unseen (Snap Gun, Chapter 3), 5 bank vaults (Chapter 4), an armored truck, and one illegal operation per gang (9 gangs, 39 operations).

    Earns: #15–#21, #28, #30–#32, #35, #39–#41, #43, #46

  4. Phase 4 — The grinds Free-roam after the story

    Everything left is countable, so grind it after the credits. 250 stunts (300 for 100%), 50 challenges (70 for 100%), 500 actions that change your Criminal Profile, $250,000 of marked bills laundered, $100,000 spent in shops, all 8 garages, 10 vehicles registered. Open the 100% checklist under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work it down — it is forgiving, about 60% true completion.

    Earns: #14, #15, #32, #48, #52–#55 · plus the cryptic five (#44, #45, #49–#51) once their methods are confirmed

  5. Phase 5 — Platinum Vice Got a Price!

    When the other 54 are done, the Platinum pops automatically. There is no extra task, no difficulty trophy and no online trophy standing between you and it.

    Earns: #1

Sources & honesty notes

Official names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, cross-checked 19 November 2026 against the lists on planetgta6.com (taken from Exophase, cross-checked with PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements), OutputLag and PowerPyx — all three agree on every name, description and grade. Every guide above is written in our own words for this entry and names its sources beside it; nothing is copied from another guide. Where a method is still unconfirmed at launch (#44, #45, #49, #50, #51), the guide says so instead of inventing one. One reported count could not be verified (the number of regions for #42) and is not asserted. The game launched 19 November 2026, so launch-week guides are the source of record and this page will age — re-check the cited guides before trusting a single detail.

No Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshot is used here; the look is our own. This guide works fully offline: it loads only its own files and makes no network request. Progress is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves it except when you export it yourself.
